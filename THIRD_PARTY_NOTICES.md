# Third-party notices

## Supplied photographic references

The cotton color and relief textures are derived from an unprinted portion of the [Hoban Patrick Bateman product photograph](https://hobancards.com/cdn/shop/products/patrick_bateman_2_1728x.jpg?v=1579734013), supplied as the paper reference. The material simulates Reich SAVOY Natural White cotton stock and the recessed black letterpress impression.

The favicon uses the American Psycho close-up supplied by the user after the [Jukebox article reference](https://www.jukeboxprint.com/blog/american-psycho-business-card). The original photographs belong to their respective owners.

## Supplied audio

The opening and reveal cues are the user's supplied `card comes out.m4a` and `reaction to card on hover.m4a`, copied unchanged to `public/audio/`. The recordings belong to their respective owners.

## Cursor reference

The cursor follows [Nachi's](https://www.nachi.design/) immediate pointer tracking and difference blending. The pixel arrow and hand artwork are drawn locally for this site.

## Holo

The pointer normalization and follow behavior in `src/components/BusinessScene.tsx` draw on Arlan's published Holo engine: https://www.arlan.me/vault/holo. Its source is marked MIT on that page. The implementation adapts this approach to Three.js meshes and frame-rate-independent damping.
